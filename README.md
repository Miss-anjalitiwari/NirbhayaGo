# NirbhayaGo
NirbhayaGo is a women-only travel safety and companion platform that connects women with verified female travel service providers and potential travel partners through smart route and time-based matching.
#NirbhayaGo – Travel Safer, Travel Together

NirbhayaGo is a women-focused travel safety and companion platform designed to make travelling safer, easier, and more comfortable through trusted female connections.

The platform offers two core features:

### 1. Book a Female Travel Provider
Women can discover and book registered female service providers for travel assistance, making their journeys more comfortable and convenient.

### 2. Find Your Travel Partner
Women travelling to the same or nearby destinations at similar times can discover each other, connect, and choose to travel together.

### Key Features
- Female-focused travel community.
- Female travel service provider registration and booking.
- Smart route and time-based travel partner matching.
- User profiles and travel details.
- Safety-focused features and trusted connections.
# Our Vision

To create a trusted women-only travel ecosystem where every woman can find travel assistance or a companion and travel with greater confidence.
**NirbhayaGo – Because every journey deserves confidence.**
